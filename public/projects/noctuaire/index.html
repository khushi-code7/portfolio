02Materials

Bought once, at harvest.

Every material below is bought in a single lot, once a year, and the whole of that year's production is macerated together in one vessel in Grasse. What comes out is what there is. When a batch is finished it is finished, and the next will not smell identical, because the orris will not be identical.

03The House

We do not dilute.

Noctuaire makes four fragrances. Each is sold as extrait and only as extrait — there is no eau de parfum, no eau de toilette, no summer edition. A dilution is a different fragrance wearing the same name, and we would rather you knew which one you had bought.

The house was founded in 2016 by two people who had spent a decade reformulating other people's briefs downward, and wanted to see what happened if nobody was allowed to ask for it cheaper. The atelier is four rooms above a former glazier's on Rue Amiral de Grasse. The laboratory is one of them.

We publish the lot table above because we think you should be able to see what you are paying for, and because a house that will not tell you where its orris comes from usually has a reason.

04The Ritual

How to wear an extrait.

Extrait is not eau de parfum with a higher price. It is a different material and it wants to be worn differently — sparingly, close to the body, and left alone once applied.

  1. To the pulse, never the cloth

    Wrist, the hollow of the throat, behind the ear. Fabric holds an extrait far too long and gives none of it back, which is how a scarf ends up smelling of it a season later.

  2. One point, not three

    A single application is the whole dose. At 28% and above, the second point is not twice as much fragrance — it is the same fragrance, worn louder than it was built to be worn.

  3. Do not rub

    Rubbing shears the top notes off and starts the drydown early. Apply it, and then leave it. The first fifteen minutes are the part most people destroy.

Noctuaire is not a real company. It is a front-end demonstration built by Khushi Bajaj. Every fragrance, address, material claim and price on this page is invented.

The liquid is one fragment shader. There is no 3D model and no animation library: nine spheres are blended with a polynomial smooth‑minimum into a signed distance field and raymarched per pixel, at verts 0 · tris 0 · draw calls 1. The lobes are springs on the CPU — anchored to slow orbits, pulled toward the pointer at different rates, and damped, which is what makes the mass neck and drip rather than slide as one piece. Each fragrance changes the blend radius, so the four behave differently rather than merely differing in colour. The bottles are one SVG symbol; on the product page the same outline clips the live canvas, so the extrait moves inside the glass.

No dependencies, no webfonts, no build step. It renders a still frame under prefers-reduced-motion, drops resolution when frames run long, stops entirely when scrolled out of view, and falls back to a painted gradient where WebGL is unavailable.
