I care about interfaces that feel obvious in use and hold up under maintenance.
Most of my work sits between design and engineering: turning a rough idea into
something concrete, then making the concrete thing fast, accessible, and boring
to maintain.

## What I work on

- Front-end architecture for content-heavy sites and web apps
- Design systems — the components, and the rules that keep them coherent
- Performance and accessibility as constraints, not clean-up tasks

## How I work

I like small, legible codebases. Few dependencies, plain HTML where plain HTML
will do, and tests on the parts that actually contain decisions. I would rather
ship something narrow and solid than something broad and half-wired.
