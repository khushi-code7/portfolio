## A note on the comparison

The mechanism in this piece depends on a comparison a shopper never makes, which
means the interesting question is not why the prices differ but why the gap goes
unnoticed. Placeholder text where that argument would be developed: the physical
distance between the two products, the different words printed on them, and the

A second paragraph on what would count as evidence against the claim. If the gap
closed in stores that placed the two products side by side, that would support
it; if the gap persisted regardless of placement, the explanation would have to
be something other than the aisle, and the piece would need rewriting rather
than defending.
