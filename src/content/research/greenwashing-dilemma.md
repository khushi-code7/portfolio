Firms have worked out that environmental claims sell. Rather less has been
settled about what happens when consumers stop believing them — whether the
damage stays with the brand that overclaimed, or spreads to sustainable
products generally.

This paper surveyed 100 urban Indian consumers and combined the quantitative
results with qualitative responses, looking at three things: what greenwashing
does to **consumer trust**, what it does to **brand equity**, and whether it
changes **sustainable buying behaviour**. It closes with recommendations on
both the policy and the branding side.

Presented at ICMBP-2025 at Aliah University, Kolkata, where I was the youngest
presenter at the conference.
